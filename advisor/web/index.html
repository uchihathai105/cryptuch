Watch: coins with big swings

Type any Binance USDT coin. Each one always shows a side: Long / Short on futures and Buy / Sell on spot (Sell means sell if you hold it, or stay out). The side comes from a trend score (−100 to +100) on 5-minute candles. Ranging coins are traded against the edge of their range, trending coins with the trend. Every side change is graded so you can see whether the rules really work.

Do the signals work?

Every side change is a prediction with a stop and two targets from the 5m ATR (stop 1 ATR; T1 1R takes half; T2 2R). The official result is at 1 hour. A signal still open then is followed until 2 hours with the same stop and targets, which are never moved. Results are in R after fees and slippage. A group needs at least 30 signals before its numbers mean anything.
